| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| latest  | :white_check_mark: |

## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security vulnerability, **please do not open a public issue**.  
Instead, report it to us securely:

- Email: [SECURITY-CONTACT-EMAIL]
- (Optional) Encrypted channel: [Provide PGP key or other method]

### Responsible Disclosure
- Do not share details publicly until we have confirmed and released a fix.
- Give us a reasonable amount of time (e.g., 30 days) to investigate and patch.
- We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and provide status updates.

### Out of Scope
The following are not considered vulnerabilities:
- Denial-of-service attacks that require large resource consumption.
- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies not maintained by us.
- Social engineering or phishing attacks targeting project maintainers.

## Our Commitment
- We will investigate every report thoroughly.
- We will release fixes as quickly as possible.
- We will credit security researchers who follow responsible disclosure guidelines.
