# 🔐 Security Policy

This project contains proprietary and sensitive components.  
We take security very seriously. Please read the following policy carefully.  

---

## 📌 Supported Versions
Only the **latest production release** is maintained and supported.  
Older versions are **not** eligible for security updates.

| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| latest  | ✅ Yes             |
| others  | ❌ No              |

---

## 📢 Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security vulnerability, **DO NOT open a public issue or discussion**.  
Public disclosure of vulnerabilities is strictly prohibited.  

Instead, report vulnerabilities through one of the following **private and secure channels**:

- 📧 Email: **[security@company.com]** *(preferred)*  
- 🔒 Encrypted Channel: PGP key available upon request  

⚠️ **Any attempt to leak, sell, or disclose vulnerabilities without authorization will be treated as a breach of trust and may involve legal consequences.**

---

## ✅ Responsible Disclosure Guidelines
By reporting a vulnerability, you agree to the following:

1. Do not share details with anyone outside the security response team.  
2. Allow us a minimum of **30 days** to validate and deploy a fix before any disclosure.  
3. Provide detailed reproduction steps, logs, or proof-of-concept when reporting.  
4. Respect confidentiality — this project may involve corporate-sensitive data.  

---

## ❌ Out of Scope
The following are **not considered vulnerabilities** under this policy:  

- Denial-of-Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks requiring excessive resource usage  
- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies outside our control  
- Issues requiring physical access to developer or production systems  
- Social engineering, phishing, or other attacks targeting individual maintainers  

---

## 🛡 Our Commitment
- We will **acknowledge your report within 48 hours**.  
- We will **provide updates** as we investigate and resolve the issue.  
- We will **release patches promptly**, prioritizing critical issues.  
- We will **credit valid reports** if disclosure is handled responsibly.  

---

## ⚠️ Confidentiality & Legal Notice
This repository and its contents are protected. Unauthorized use, reproduction, or disclosure of vulnerabilities may result in **disciplinary, contractual, or legal action** in accordance with applicable laws and company policy.
